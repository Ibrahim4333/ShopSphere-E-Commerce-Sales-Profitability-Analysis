Key insights

  • Profit is concentrated: Laptops deliver 41.6% of profit; the top 3 products (Laptop, Smartphone, Tablet) deliver 74%.
  • Margins are tight (26.9%–28.2%): growth comes from volume and mix, not pricing gaps. Tablet is the one high-revenue, below-average-margin product.
  • Seasonality: March is the revenue peak (₹60.4M) and October the low (₹48.7M).
  • Leakage risk: 32.7% of orders are Cancelled or Returned. Investigate with fulfilment and refund data.
  • Discounts: margin stays ~27.3–27.6% at every discount level. This is descriptive only, not causal.
Data note: synthetic dataset. Revenue and profit are populated for all order statuses (including Cancelled and Returned), so totals are not verified net sales. Figures come from the cleaned dataset (14,980 rows after removing 50 duplicates and 20 invalid quantities); 50 orders with an unknown city are excluded from the city chart. Monthly profit is derived from each month's reported margin.
